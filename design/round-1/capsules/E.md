# E

**Premise.** No single frame identifies anyone. The pipeline links detections across
time, averages a track's crops into one vector, and clusters fragments back together —
so identity is an accumulation, and any individual attempt is weak evidence. The page
states the same sentence repeatedly, each attempt smaller and fainter than the last,
until the accumulation rather than any one line becomes the statement that holds.

**Fonts.** Erode (Indian Type Foundry; Nikhil Ranganathan) alone, weights 300–700 with
italics. ITF Free Font License — free for personal and commercial use, no purchase, no
account. Served from api.fontshare.com. Fallback: Georgia.

**Type roles.** Six graded attempt levels, each a real weight and size step:
2.75rem/600, 2.125rem/500, 1.625rem/400, 1.25rem/400 italic, 1rem/400 italic,
0.8125rem/300 italic — with line-height rising 1.16 → 1.60 and tracking loosening
−0.024em → +0.004em as presence falls. Resolution = Erode 700 at
`clamp(2.5rem, 6.4vw, 4.75rem)`, line-height 1.02, tracking −0.034em, max 16ch. Plain
statements = 500 at 1.125rem. Figures = 600 at 0.9375rem, tabular numerals.

**Case.** Sentence case everywhere except the roles line, uppercase at 0.28em.

**Colour.** Canvas #FFFDF8 (flat, not counted). Two active letterform colours.
Secondary #B9AFA1 carries every attempt — the six repetitions, the repeated capture
messages, the three verdict words, the angle row, the retention statement: roughly
55% of visible text, and the larger share by design. Primary #2A2622 is reserved for
what survived accumulation — the resolution, the proposition, the successful capture
line, the name, both claims, the figures: roughly 45%. The faint colour is the
majority; that is the point.

**Space.** Single narrow column, max 52rem, padding 6rem / 2.5rem / 9rem. Attempts
have zero margin between them — they stack as one accumulating mass. Three intervals
separate masses: 2.5rem, 5rem, 8.5rem. The resolution takes 3.5rem of clearance above
it and nothing below.

**Signature relationship.** A graded repetition ladder in one typeface, where size,
weight, style, line-height and tracking all decay together, so the six lines read as
one fading series rather than six separate sizes.

**Invariants.** One typeface. The six-step decay with all five axes moving together.
Faint outnumbering held. Zero margin inside an accumulation.

**Allowed variation.** Number of decay steps, which sentence accumulates.

**Prohibited normalization.** Adding a second typeface. Spacing the attempts evenly
apart. Making the faint colour more legible than the held one.

**Assumptions and risks.** The faintest steps are deliberately near the edge of
comfortable reading; they are texture, and any text a user must act on has to be
promoted out of the ladder in Round 2.

**Round 2 translation.** Enrolment progress and video processing both accumulate — the
ladder is the natural form for "samples so far" and "frames sampled". Never use it for
a final decision, which must be set at the held level.
